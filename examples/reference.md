## Refunds

Refund requests are accepted within 30 days of purchase. Customers must provide a receipt.
Approved refunds go back to the original payment method. The document does not specify processing fees.

### Exceptions

Opened software and personalized items cannot be returned. Damaged items remain eligible for review.

## Delivery

Standard shipping takes 5 business days after dispatch. Express shipping takes 2 business days.
Orders placed during a weekend are dispatched on the next business day.

## Warranty

The warranty covers manufacturing defects for 12 months. Accidental damage is excluded.
Customers must provide a receipt when requesting a warranty repair.
